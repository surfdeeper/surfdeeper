The seafloor controls how incoming energy transforms into breaking waves:

- Point: rock headlands that refract and line up swell into long, peeling waves
- Reef: fixed contour (rock/coral) that creates consistent takeoff zones and hollower shapes
- Beach: movable sandbars that change with swell, wind, and tide, producing variable peaks

Small depth changes = big differences in speed, peel angle, and sectioning.
