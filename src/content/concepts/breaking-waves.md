As swell enters shallow water, it slows, steepens, and eventually breaks. Three key processes:

1. Shoaling: wave base touches bottom; speed decreases, height increases, wavelength shortens
2. Refraction: segments over shallower contours slow more and bend toward shallows, focusing energy
3. Breaking criterion: when steepness exceeds a threshold, the crest outruns the base and topples

Outcomes depend on slope and texture:

- Gentle slope + wind chop → spilling/rolling
- Steeper step or ledge → plunging/throwing
- Very steep beachface → surging with minimal curl

Use these cues to choose takeoff zones and anticipate sections.
