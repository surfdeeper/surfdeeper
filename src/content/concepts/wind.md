Wind stacks or smooths the ocean’s surface:

- Offshore: supports the face, hollows the lip, improves shape (especially at lower tides)
- Onshore: adds chop, crumbles lips, reduces line speed; manageable with more period/size
- Grooming: morning glass, sea breezes, and diurnal patterns vary by coastline

Watch flags, smoke, and surface texture to anticipate changes while you’re in the water.
