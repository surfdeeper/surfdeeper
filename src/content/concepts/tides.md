Tides change water depth over reefs, bars, and channels, turning spots on or off. Learn:

- Stages: low, mid, high; and the transitions between them
- Range: spring vs. neap — larger ranges amplify changes in wave shape and current
- Local effect: sandbar exposure, backwash, and shore break intensity

Tip: log your sessions with tide stage to find your spot’s “magic window.”
