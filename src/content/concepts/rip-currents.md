Rips form where water escaping the surf zone funnels through deeper gaps in bars or along structures. Look for:

- Darker, textured lanes with fewer breaking waves
- Outward‑moving foam, sediment, or surface chop
- Sand plumes beyond the lineup

Usage and safety:

- Smart assist for paddling out — if it’s not pushing into hazards
- Escape by floating and angling to the side; don’t sprint directly against it
