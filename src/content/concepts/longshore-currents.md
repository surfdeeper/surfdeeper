When waves approach at an angle, the push of broken water drives a current parallel to shore. Effects:

- Drifts surfers down the beach and reshapes sandbars over time
- Feeds rips where the alongshore flow converges into channels
- Intensifies with larger tide ranges and wind‑driven setup

Plan entries and exits with the drift in mind; pick land markers to track movement.
