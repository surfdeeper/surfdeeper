Swell is wind energy transferred to the ocean over distance and time. Key variables:

- Period (s): longer period = deeper energy, faster, more powerful waves
- Direction (°): where the swell is arriving from; local spots are aspect‑dependent
- Height (m/ft): the energy expressed at the buoy — transformed by bathymetry near shore

What to look for: combine period, direction, and local bathymetry to predict size and quality at your break.
