## A compact overview

SurfDeeper is a knowledge web, not a textbook. Concepts connect via relationships (dependsOn, leadsTo, appliesTo) and can be viewed through lenses (levels, paths). This lets us offer linear paths for beginners, cross-sections by topic and skill, and open exploration for deeper learning — without rigid outlines.
