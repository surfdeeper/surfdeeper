Good paddling posture keeps your board trimming flat and your shoulders happy.

- Chest just behind center; chin up, eyes forward
- Neutral spine; avoid over-arching the lower back
- Legs together and quiet to minimize drag
- Gentle hip pressure to keep the nose from pearling

Focus on feeling the board plane rather than forcing it down into the water.
