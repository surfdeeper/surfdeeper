# Surf Stance

## Overview

Your stance is how you position your feet, knees, and body on the board once you’ve popped up. Good stance gives you balance, control, and the ability to turn.

## Key Points

- Back foot: perpendicular to the stringer, roughly over the tail pad or just ahead of the fins on longer boards
- Front foot: about a 45° angle relative to the stringer
- Feet: shoulder-width apart (adjust for board size), with weight centered between them
- Knees: soft bend; avoid locking out
- Upper body: chest facing slightly forward, eyes looking where you want to go

## Getting Into Stance

If you’re working on your pop-up, establish space with a solid setup first — see [Cobra Pose](:cobra-pose) — then land your feet into this stance.

## Practice

- Land-drills: Mark foot spots on the floor and practice popping into them
- On small waves or whitewater: focus on landing feet first, then settling your weight
