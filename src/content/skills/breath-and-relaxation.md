Breath control anchors your paddling rhythm and keeps tension low.

- Inhale at hand entry; exhale through the pull
- Keep the jaw, neck, and hands relaxed
- Use slow nasal breathing between sets to reset

Practice pairing breath with motion until it feels automatic.
