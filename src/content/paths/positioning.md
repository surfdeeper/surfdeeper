## Components

- Peak-relative positioning: outside/inside, left/right, deeper/shallower
- Dynamic adjustment: paddle half-steps, angle changes, and micro-drifts
- Priority and etiquette: catch more waves without cutting others off
- Linking: positioning feeds paddling, angle, and the takeoff sequence

## Payoff

Small, constant adjustments mean fewer missed chances and more clean entries—without burning extra energy.
