## What you’ll learn

- Swell, tide, wind: how they combine to shape today’s waves
- Sandbars and reefs: where energy focuses and how peaks shift
- Rips and channels: use them to move, avoid them to stay safe
- Signals: body-language of the crowd, interval timing, and changes during your session

## Why it matters

Better ocean reading means fewer late paddles, cleaner entries, and more makeable waves—with less stress and safer decisions.
