## What this path covers

- Mechanics: stroke length vs. rate, catch, exit, and timing with the wave
- Posture: ribcage position, neutral neck, and shoulder-friendly movement
- Conditioning: sustainable pace, intervals, and breath control
- Strategy: when to sprint, when to cruise, and how to conserve energy
- Drills: short, focused practices you can repeat and measure

## Outcomes

- Paddle farther with less fatigue
- Hold a higher average speed without spiking effort
- Arrive in the right spot earlier and calmer
- Keep shoulders healthy for the long haul
