Core skills are the bedrock that make everything else work better.

Start with your equipment. [[board-care-and-repair|Board care & repair]] keeps your boards watertight, fins secure, and wax right for the day. Well‑maintained gear feels predictable underfoot so you can focus on reading the wave, not fighting your setup.

Next, build the engine of your surfing: direction changes. [[bottom-and-top-turns|Bottom and top turns]] are the rail‑to‑rail fundamentals that set your speed and trajectory. A clean bottom turn loads the rail and projects you up the face; a controlled top turn resets your line without stalling.

When sections run ahead or you drift wide, learn to reconnect with power. [[cutbacks|Cutbacks]] bring you from the shoulder back to the pocket, linking one high‑quality line into the next. Think smooth arcs, held rail, and a rebound that keeps you moving.

With line control in place, use the wave’s slope to travel. [[angling-down-the-line|Angling down the line]] teaches you to draw clean lines that match the wave’s speed—high when you need glide, lower when you want to tap into push.

Finally, turn clean lines into acceleration. [[trimming-and-speed|Trimming for speed]] is about small weight shifts, subtle rail engagement, and timing—micro‑adjustments that keep you in the pocket and make everything else easier.
