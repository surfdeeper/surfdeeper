## Core skills

- Read sets and lulls to time your paddle-outs
- Choose lines that minimize impact and maximize progress
- Use turtle rolls or duck dives appropriately for your board and wave size
- Recover quickly after impact and continue efficiently

## Simple progression

1. Start on smaller days to build pattern recognition
2. Practice angle changes and micro-rests between surges
3. Add fast bursts through reform sections
4. Graduate to steeper days with confident timing
