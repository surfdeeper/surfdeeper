Understanding the ocean is the fastest way to level up your surfing. This path covers how waves are created, shaped, and steered by the environment — then connects that knowledge to what you see from the beach and feel in the water.

What you’ll learn along the way:

- Where swell comes from and how period, direction, and energy affect your local break
- How wind (offshore vs. onshore) sculpts the face and changes paddle and positioning strategy
- Why tide stage and range can turn a spot on or off
- How the seafloor (sand, rock, reef; point vs. beach vs. reef) focuses or disperses energy
- The difference between wave “types” and the mechanics of how waves actually break
- How currents (rips and longshore drift) move water — and how to use them or avoid them
