The takeoff isn’t a mystery — it’s a sequence. Build entry speed, feel the release into glide, add two honest strokes, free the nose, set your line, then stand.

## Your Timing Model

1. Match the wave with efficient [paddling](:paddling-efficiency)
2. Keep the nose free with a [micro‑cobra](:cobra-pose) as lift appears
3. Feel the [glide/planing](:gliding-planing) — that’s your green‑light
4. Begin slight [angling](:angling-down-the-line) during the last stroke
5. If it steepens, actively [avoid pearling](:avoid-pearling)
6. Commit to a decisive [pop‑up](:pop-up)
7. Find [trim and speed](:trimming-and-speed)

Use small, predictable waves to wire the sequence. Then tighten the windows as conditions get steeper or quicker.
