## Step it up

- [[read-the-ocean]] — Identify peaks, rips, channels, and shifting sections
- [[wave-selection]] — Choose makeable waves, pass on closeouts
- [[steeper-takeoffs]] — Commit earlier, angle in, and stay high
- [[angling-down-the-line]] — Set your line to keep speed and make sections
- [[trimming-and-speed]] — Manage height and rail for flow
- [[linking-maneuvers]] — Connect bottom/top turns fluidly
- [[video-review]] — Capture a few reps, review, and set next focus

## Training notes

Alternate focus days: (1) Positioning/Selection, (2) Takeoff/Angle, (3) Trim/Linking. Keep notes after each session.
